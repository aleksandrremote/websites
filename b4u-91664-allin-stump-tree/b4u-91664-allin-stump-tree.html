About Allin Stump & Tree

Allin Stump & Tree is Mike Allin's crew, and in Concord they've built a reputation for taking on the jobs other companies stall on: four large trees dropped in a single day, seven trees removed inches from a homeowner's foundation, stumps ground down eight inches below grade with a small loader that never chews up the yard.

What keeps people calling Mike back isn't just the tree work, it's how little evidence is left behind when they're done. Homeowners consistently mention the same thing: quotes that show up same-day, jobs that start when promised instead of two months later, and a crew that clears brush, protects the grass, and leaves the property cleaner than they found it.

Common questions

How deep do you grind stumps?

We grind stumps a full eight inches below grade so you can replant or landscape right over the spot without hitting wood.

Will your equipment tear up my lawn?

No. We use a small loader for cleanup and take extra care with vehicle placement so your grass stays intact, even on jobs with multiple large trees.

How fast can you get someone out for a quote?

We typically respond the same day you call, and most jobs are scheduled and completed within a couple of weeks.
