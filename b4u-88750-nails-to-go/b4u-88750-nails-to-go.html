About Nails To Go

Nails To Go is Katherine's own shop in Danbury, built around one idea: you shouldn't have to settle for a rough copy of the design you had in mind. Show her a photo and she matches it stroke for stroke, whether that's a full art set, a clean gel manicure, or a pedicure meant to last.

What keeps clients coming back on repeat isn't just the artwork, it's how the whole visit feels. People mention the same things again and again: a spotless station, tools she visibly sanitizes, and a conversation that makes an hour in the chair feel easy. Add in pedicures that still look sharp weeks later and it's easy to see why so many first-timers become regulars.

Common questions

Can I bring a photo of the design I want?

Yes, that's exactly how Katherine likes to work. Bring any picture and she'll match the design, shape, and color as closely as possible.

Do you take walk-ins or is it appointment only?

Booking ahead is best since it's a one-chair studio, but call for the day you need and Katherine will do her best to fit you in.

What are your hours on Saturday?

Saturdays run 8 AM to 5 PM, earlier than the weekday 2 to 8 PM schedule, so it's the easiest day to grab a morning slot. The shop is closed Sundays.
