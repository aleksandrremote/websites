About Pool Rescue Squad

Pool Rescue Squad is the Atlanta crew people call after they've already tried someone else and gotten nowhere. Dustin and Kile handle everything from full liner replacements and underwater lighting to pump systems fried by lightning, and they quote jobs honestly instead of charging just to show up.

What keeps customers coming back is how fast and how straight they work. A liner swap that most companies stretch into a week gets done in under 24 hours, a green, algae-choked pool that sat dead for years gets brought back to swimmable, and every job comes with a real explanation of what broke and why, not a bill with no answers attached.
