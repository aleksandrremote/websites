About Man VS Treez

Man VS Treez is run by Daniel and a crew of climbers who genuinely like the work, the kind of guys who show up, walk your lot, and hand you a proposal with drone photos and real tree heights before you've even finished your coffee. They take down the massive stuff, the small stuff, and the pile you cut and stacked yourself two summers ago and never got around to hauling.

Customers who've gotten three bids keep landing on the same conclusion: Daniel's price is fair, his paperwork is straight, and his crew treats your property like it's theirs. They don't just drop a tree and leave, they rake, they blow out the gutters, they clean the neighbor's yard too if a branch drifted that direction, and they've been known to come back and treat a sick tree with insecticide after a full day's work on someone else's job.

Common questions

Do you actually provide a real quote before showing up with a chainsaw?

Yes. Daniel walks the property first and sends over a written proposal with drone photos and approximate tree heights, so you know exactly what you're paying for before anyone climbs anything.

Will there be a mess left in my yard afterward?

No. The crew hauls away all the wood, chips the debris, and cleans up thoroughly, including a neighbor's yard if any branches ended up over the fence.

Are you insured in case something goes wrong?

Yes, and Daniel will send over proof of insurance as soon as you ask, no chasing him down for it.
