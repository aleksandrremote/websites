About ZRamirez Tile And Stone

ZRamirez Tile and Stone is run by Ingrid and Luis, a husband-and-wife team who treat every shower, backsplash, and floor like it's going in their own house. Luis is the one clients call an artisan, the guy who can take a difficult custom cut and make it look like it was born that way, from frameless glass shower surrounds to jetted tubs and full sauna installs.

What clients keep coming back to is what happens when things get complicated. When a shower demo uncovers old water damage behind the wall, they stop, fix the structure properly including the boards that support a glass door, and finish the job right instead of tiling over a problem. Estimates come in fair, timelines are honest, and they show up when they say they will, which is why so many of their jobs start with a single small request and end with a homeowner asking what else they can redo.

Common questions

What happens if you find damage behind our shower wall once demo starts?

We stop and repair it properly first, including structural boards, before any new tile or glass goes in, so the finished shower is solid, not just pretty.

Can you work from a tile we already picked out, or do you help design the layout too?

Both. Bring us a tile you found at the store or online and we'll handle the layout, cuts, and installation to match what you pictured.

How fast can you get a project started and finished?

Smaller jobs like a backsplash can often be scheduled and completed the same day you call, while full remodels get a clear timeline upfront so you know what to expect.
