About Crystal Clean Car Wash

Crystal Clean Car Wash runs brushless, touchless automatic bays alongside full self-serve stalls, so you can choose a hands-off wash or dial in your own rinse, wash, and wax exactly how you like it. Every bay is stocked and running 24 hours a day, which is why regulars treat this as their go-to spot instead of bouncing between the three or four other washes nearby.

Common questions

What's the difference between the automatic wash and the self-serve bays?

The automatic wash is a touchless, brushless cycle that runs your whole car for you, while the self-serve bays let you hand-control rinse, wash, and wax on your own time.

What if the automatic wash doesn't trigger or start correctly?

Someone is on-site to help you get the cycle restarted at no extra cost, which is a common reason regulars stick with us over other unmanned washes nearby.

Are you open late or overnight?

Yes, every bay and the automatic wash run 24 hours a day, seven days a week, so you can wash your car whenever it fits your schedule.
