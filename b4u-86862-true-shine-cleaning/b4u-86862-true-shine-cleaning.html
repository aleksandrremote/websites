About True Shine Cleaning

True Shine Cleaning is Mariana's crew, and they've been working homes and small offices around Newark for close to a decade. Every new client starts with a full deep clean, and after that they move to a custom checklist for standard visits, weekly, biweekly, or whatever schedule fits your house, so nothing gets skipped and nothing gets overdone.

Clients stick around because Mariana actually listens. She checks in, asks for feedback, and adjusts the checklist until it matches exactly how you live. Her crew moves fast without cutting corners, they're typically in and out inside an hour, and if a garage or storage closet has spiraled out of control, they'll sort, organize, and clean that too.
