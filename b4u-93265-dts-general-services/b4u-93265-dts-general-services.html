About DTS General Services

DTS General Services is a Lemon Grove crew built around one idea: do the work like it's your own house. Ray and his team take on everything from full interior repaints and garage upgrades to custom build-outs, like framing a fireplace surround, to plumbing repairs and appliance installs that need to happen the same day.

What customers notice first is how little they have to think about once the job starts. Floors and furniture get covered before a single tool comes out, questions get answered without the runaround, and the crew checks back in after the work is done to make sure everything holds up. That's why the same names keep showing up in project after project, from a repainted living room to a new front door to a washer hookup on a Saturday.
