About Smashey & Sons Tree Service

Smashey & Sons has been dropping dangerous trees and cleaning up after storms around Creve Coeur for years, with Quinton leading crews that tackle the leaners, the widow-makers, and the massive oaks nobody else wants to touch. We handle everything from a single hazardous tree hanging over a roofline to full lot clearing, and we bring enough hands to finish in a day instead of a week.

Customers notice the same thing every time: the crew moves fast, drops limbs exactly where they're supposed to, and treats the yard like it's their own. We've reset fences, tightened up gates, and swept every last twig off the lawn, because for us the job isn't done until the ground looks like we were never there.
