About Killian's Lawn Care

Killian's Lawn Care is run by Joe, a landscape design specialist who's built a 4.9-star reputation across Moore for straight talk and sharp work. He handles everything from weekly mowing to pre and post emergent weed treatments, flowerbed installs, and full custom Christmas light setups, and he's not afraid to steer you toward the less expensive option if that's what your yard actually needs.

Customers keep coming back season after season because Joe treats every yard like it's the only one on his schedule. He'll tell you straight if your grass doesn't need cutting this week, walks you through the reasoning behind every treatment plan, and makes paying for it simple with invoicing you can handle right from your phone.
