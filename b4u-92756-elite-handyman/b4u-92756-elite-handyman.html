About Elite Handyman

Elite Handyman is the crew Pinecrest homeowners call when something needs building, mounting, or fixing correctly the first time. Alfredo, Joseph, and Franco have hung TVs, built out closets, assembled furniture down to bunk beds and grills, and installed everything from ceiling fans to deadbolts, all while holding a perfect 5-star record across 23 reviews.

Book a job and you get a price upfront that doesn't move once the work starts, a crew that shows up when they say they will, and a work area left cleaner than they found it. They've taken calls late at night and even worked on the Fourth of July, because a loose banister or an unmounted TV doesn't wait for convenient hours.
