Common questions

What animals do you trap?

Raccoons and possums are the most common calls in DeBary, along with other nuisance wildlife getting into attics and yards. This company does not handle dogs or cats.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. Patrick will come out, check your attic and roofline for active signs of animals, and identify entry points before you pay for anything.

What happens to the animal once it's trapped?

It's relocated safely to a wildlife reserve well away from your property, not just dumped down the street to become someone else's problem.
