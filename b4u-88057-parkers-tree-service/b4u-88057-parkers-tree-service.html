About Parker's Tree Service

Parker's Tree Service has built its name in Ocala on jobs most crews would rather skip: 80-foot oaks growing straight over rooftops, tight backyards packed with equipment, and multi-tree clearing projects that take grapple trucks two full loads to haul away. Mr. Parker runs the crew himself alongside team members like Kirk and Quentin, and between them they've handled everything from routine oak and palm grooming to full-property clearing ahead of a new build.

Customers keep coming back to one thing: the job gets done exactly as promised, and the property looks better after the crew leaves than before they arrived. That means branches dropped inches from a roofline without a scratch on it, weekend emergency calls answered when a tree comes down on a shed, and pricing options that flex if a customer wants to handle their own debris cleanup. It's the kind of tree work that earns a five-star rating from 84 different Ocala homeowners and business owners, not just one.

Common questions

A tree fell on my property over the weekend. Can you actually come out?

Yes. Parker's has shown up on short notice for storm damage, including a Saturday afternoon call for a tree leaning on a customer's shed, and can even adjust pricing if you handle your own debris cleanup.

Will taking down a tree near my building or roof cause damage?

No. The crew works branch by branch in tight spaces, and customers have specifically noted that overhanging trees were removed from above buildings without a single mark left on the structure.

I have several large trees to clear for a construction project. Can you handle that scale?

Yes. Parker's has cleared 80-foot trees from residential lots using two full grapple truck loads per tree and returns to finish the job in phases as a build progresses.
