About Tommy Cupp Remodeling & Painting

Tommy Cupp Remodeling & Painting is a Shreveport general contracting crew that handles the projects most companies split into three separate calls: bathroom and kitchen remodels, full home additions with plumbing and electrical, custom cabinetry and vanities, and interior painting down to the trim and texture work. Tommy walks every homeowner through material choices before a hammer ever swings, so you know exactly what you're getting and why.

What keeps people calling him back for the next job is simple: his crews show up when they say they will, they protect your home while they're in it, and the price you agree on at the start is the price you pay at the end, even when a job turns up surprises like hidden subfloor damage. Realtors preparing homes for sale rely on him for the same reason homeowners do, because the work holds up and the timeline holds too.
