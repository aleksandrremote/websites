About Vera Construction

Vera Construction is Josh's crew, and around Woodburn they're the name that keeps coming up when someone needs a roof torn off and replaced correctly, a rotted beam pulled and rebuilt, or an old farmhouse with mismatched siding brought back to one clean look. Josh shows up himself, takes photos of what he finds, and writes a scope of work that actually matches the house in front of him instead of a generic template.

People hire Josh once for a roof and end up calling him back for the closet addition, the egress windows, the ag building. That pattern shows up again and again in how customers talk about him: quotes that hold even when the job gets complicated, a crew that moves fast without cutting corners, and a guy who answers his phone and explains the options before making the call for you.

Common questions

Can you tear off and replace a roof on short notice before a weather window closes?

Yes, Josh has done full tear-offs and replacements on tight weather deadlines, including custom vent work for unusual rooflines.

Do you only do roofs, or can you handle other parts of the house too?

Vera Construction handles roofing, siding, gutters, additions, structural repair, egress windows, and ag buildings, so most of our roofing customers call us back for other projects.
