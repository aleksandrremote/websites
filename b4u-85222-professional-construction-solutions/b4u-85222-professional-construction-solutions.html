About Professional Construction Solutions

Professional Construction Solutions is a GAF-certified roofing contractor based in Johnstown, built around one idea: a roof project shouldn't be a mystery. Brandon and his crew handle everything from hail damage assessments to full exterior rebuilds, and they've turned insurance claims into their specialty, working directly with adjusters to get supplements approved without homeowners chasing paperwork.

Homeowners consistently mention the same thing after working with PCS: they always knew what was happening. Crews document their own work with on-roof photos, contracts spell out every material and payment milestone before a single shingle comes off, and jobs that could take a week get finished in a day and a half, cleanup included.

Common questions

Do you deal directly with my insurance company after storm damage?

Yes. We assess the damage, compare it against your insurance estimate, and stay in contact with your adjuster to get any needed supplements approved.

We're a GAF roofing systems contractor, and we'll walk you through material grades and color options before any work starts.

How long does a typical roof replacement take?

Most single-family roofs, including attached porches and garages, are completed in a day to a day and a half, with full site cleanup after.
