Broadway Locksmith & Keys runs around the clock out of Edgewater, handling everything from apartment lockouts along the waterfront to precision key cutting and lock upgrades for homes across town. Common questions

How fast can someone actually get to me if I'm locked out?

Broadway runs 24 hours a day and customers regularly report a technician arriving within 10 to 15 minutes, even during last-minute emergencies like catching a flight.

Will my new keys actually work without sticking or jamming?

Every key is cut precisely by a technician who checks the fit before you leave, so there's no second trip back because a key was cut wrong.

I rent my apartment, can you still help me get in or change the locks?

Yes, a large part of the work is apartment lockouts and rekeys for renters, and technicians are used to working directly with tenants and landlords.
