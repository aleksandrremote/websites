About Tree Care Experts

Tree Care Experts is Joshua and Jessica's business, built in Harker Heights on a simple rule: know the tree, know the ground it's growing in, and leave nothing behind but a clean yard. Joshua does the climbing, cutting, and root tracing himself, whether it's a 70-year-old live oak leaning over a roofline or hidden root runners working their way toward a foundation.

Every job starts with a straight answer about what's actually wrong and what it costs to fix, and ends with the yard cleared, holes filled, and everything hauled away, whether that's a single hazard branch or a tree already resting on the house. Customers keep booking them for the next project and keep sending friends their way, because Joshua and Jessica notice the small things nobody asked them to catch, from a leaky spigot to advice on where to plant the next tree so roots never become a problem again.

Common questions

Will you damage my fence or yard getting the tree out?

Yes, we're set up for emergency removal and work to get the tree off your home quickly and carefully without causing further damage.
