About Delgado Brothers Roofing

Delgado Brothers Roofing re-roofs homes across Douglas, replaces gutters, and installs vinyl soffit, fascia, and siding, and they're the crew neighbors call after seeing the job next door turn out right. When a competitor suggests caulking over old flashing to save a few dollars, this team replaces it properly, because a roof that has to survive Georgia heat, heavy rain, and hail season doesn't get built with shortcuts.

Homeowners keep saying the same thing in different words: the project managers, whether it's Braden walking through the first inspection or Danny and Jordan keeping the updates coming during the job, actually explain what's happening and why. Crews show up daily until the work is finished, sweep the yard clean before they leave, and match new shingles to old ones so well you'd have to ask where the repair even was.
