About JL Construction

JL Construction is Jack's company, and around Carrollton, Jack is the guy people call when something needs fixing and fixing well. He's replaced roof tiles and vent pipe seals before they turned into leaks, dug mold out of flooded basements, rebuilt front steps in stone, and framed a wheelchair ramp for a family in the middle of a crisis. Roofing, remodels, additions, insulation, storm repair, if it's part of a home, he's probably already fixed one like it.

What keeps people calling him back is simple: he tells you the truth about what a job actually needs, then does it right the first time. He's taken over half-finished porches and made them solid, replaced whole sets of windows without cutting corners, and caught problems in an attic that nobody else noticed. He's shown up for storm-damaged homes and church repairs on short notice, and more than once he's done it for free when a family needed it most.
