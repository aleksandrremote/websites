About Juan Remodeling

Juan Remodeling is run by Miguel, a Brockton general contractor who built his name on the kind of flooring work that makes people stop and stare at the seams. Whether it's blending new hardwood into an existing floor or laying luxury vinyl plank through multiple bedrooms in a single day, the work is precise, planned out, and finished clean.

Customers keep coming back to the same details: Miguel shows up when he says he will, gives you a straight answer when you have a question, and figures out the tricky parts before they become a problem, like stretching material just far enough to finish a job with none to spare. He'll move your fridge, work around furniture the movers couldn't get out, and leave the space cleaner than he found it.

Common questions

Can you match new hardwood to our existing floor?

Yes. Miguel specializes in blending new hardwood into existing floors, matching the seam so closely you can't tell where the old ends and the new begins.

Do you move furniture and appliances during a flooring job?

Yes, appliances like fridges and stoves get moved and reconnected, and the crew works around beds or furniture that can't be taken out of the room.

How long does a luxury vinyl plank install take?

A job covering multiple bedrooms and closets can typically be completed in a day or two, with careful undercutting at the baseboards for a clean finish.
