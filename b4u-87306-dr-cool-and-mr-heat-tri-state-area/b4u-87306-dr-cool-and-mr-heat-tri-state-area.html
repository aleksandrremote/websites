About Dr Cool And Mr Heat (TRI State Area)

Dr Cool And Mr Heat keeps homes across the Tri-State area comfortable through Mississippi winters and Memphis-hot summers. Based in Hernando, the team handles everything from furnace repairs and thermostat installs to full AC replacements, often the same day you call. When another company writes a unit off as beyond repair, this is the crew that finds the fix anyway.
