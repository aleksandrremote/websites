About Sharky

Sharky Pools runs on a simple idea: know pools inside and out, and treat every customer's equipment like it's your own money on the line. Owner Sean has built a reputation around Hurricane and St. George for knowing exactly how a system runs, down to the part number, and for finding ways to save homeowners money instead of upselling them.

What keeps people with Sharky year after year is the follow-through. Techs like Lyle and Chase don't just skim and brush, they hand-wash filters and clean salt cells by hand on every visit, then send an email with photos and a chemical readout so you know exactly what happened while you were at work. When something's off, Sean has been known to hop on FaceTime himself and walk a customer through it until it's fixed.
