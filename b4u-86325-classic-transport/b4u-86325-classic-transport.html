About Classic Transport

Kenny and JoAnne started Classic Transport as a real family business, and it still runs that way today. League City drivers call them for the routine stuff, a dead battery, a flat, a car that won't start in a parking lot, and they also call when the job gets complicated: a low-slung sports car that can't take a standard hook, a commercial truck that needs a specialty flatbed, or a vehicle buried axle-deep in mud.

What keeps people coming back for years, not just once, is simple: the phone gets answered, the driver who shows up knows what he's doing, and the price is what they told you it would be. Drivers like Willie and Josh handle everything from delicate exotic pickups to heavy commercial loads without drama, and the dispatch team works to get a truck moving toward you the moment you call, any hour of the day or night.
