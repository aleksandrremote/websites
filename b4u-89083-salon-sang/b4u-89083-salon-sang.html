About Salon Sang

Salon Sang is a private nail studio in Eugene run by Sang, who has spent years perfecting dip powder manicures and pedicures that actually last. There's no waiting room, no rotating techs, just one dedicated space with your own dedicated parking spot right outside the door.

Clients who've been coming for three years or more say the same thing: the color selection is huge, the grow-out lines stay smooth instead of chunky, and Sang notices the small details other techs skip. Walk in for a pedicure and you'll get a massage chair, a citrus scrub that smells incredible, and someone who genuinely pays attention to what you want before she ever picks up a tool.
