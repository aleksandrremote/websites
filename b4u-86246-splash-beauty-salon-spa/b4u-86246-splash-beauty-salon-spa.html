About SpLash Beauty Salon & Spa

SpLash Beauty Salon & Spa is Jamie's studio in Frankfort, built around one idea: color should be bold and your hair should still be healthy underneath it. Clients come in for a cut and leave with a real education on what their hair needs, which is why so many of them never book with anyone else again.

Ask around and you'll hear the same story from a bride in Lizton, a first-time color client, or someone who's been coming in for years: Jamie listens first, makes changes when something isn't quite right, and treats every face and hair type differently instead of running one routine on everybody. Whether it's a wedding morning with five different hairstyles to manage or a Tuesday afternoon color appointment, the result is the same. People leave looking like themselves, just better.

Common questions

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes. The team has traveled as far as Lizton Lodge, arrives early to set up, and works through however many hair types and looks the wedding party needs.

How does the bridal trial work?

Jamie takes detailed notes on what's working and what isn't, and she'll adjust anything on the spot so the look is locked in before the actual day.

My hair has been damaged from past color jobs. Can you fix it?

This is Jamie's specialty. She evaluates hair health first and builds a color plan around restoring it, not just covering it up.
