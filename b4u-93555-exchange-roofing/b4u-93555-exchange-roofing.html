About Exchange Roofing

Exchange Roofing is a Manvel-based roofing crew that Julio Reyes and his team built on straight talk and honest pricing. When hail or hurricane winds tear up a roof, they show up with a tarp first, no charge and no obligation, then walk the homeowner through exactly what the insurance claim will cover before a single shingle comes off.

Homeowners who've had five different companies knock on their door after a storm keep picking Exchange because Julio actually explains the process instead of rushing the signature. Crews finish most full replacements in one to two days, clean up every scrap of old material, and take the extra step of matching flashing, vents, and even shutter paint to the new roof so the whole house looks finished, not just patched.

Common questions

Will you help me deal with my insurance company after storm damage?

Yes. Julio meets with your adjuster, explains what's covered, and works directly with the insurance company to finalize the fees before any work starts.

How long does a roof replacement actually take?

Most full replacements are done in one to two days, though jobs with rotted decking that needs replacing can run a bit longer.

My roof was damaged in the last storm. Can you tarp it right away?

Yes, we tarp storm-damaged roofs free of charge with no obligation to hire us for the repair, which is exactly how many of our customers first found us.
