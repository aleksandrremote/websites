About SUPER DAN PLUMBING & HEATING

Super Dan Plumbing & Heating is a one-man operation built on a simple idea: fix what's actually broken, charge a fair price, and leave the place cleaner than you found it. Dan handles everything from clogged toilets and leaking diverters to full bathroom and kitchen plumbing, and he's the guy Elmwood Park calls when other plumbers won't touch a small job.

What keeps people calling him back isn't a sales pitch, it's the way he actually explains the problem before he touches anything. He'll ask if you need a full repair or just a stopgap before your renovation, and he'll price it accordingly instead of running up the bill. Customers consistently mention the same things: he shows up when he says he will, he uses solid parts, and he treats a small cape the same way he'd treat a mansion.
