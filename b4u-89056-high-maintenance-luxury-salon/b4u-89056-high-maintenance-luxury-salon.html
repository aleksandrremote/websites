About High Maintenance Luxury Salon

High Maintenance Luxury Salon in Danville is where clients come for color that actually blends, cuts that grow out soft instead of awkward, and lashes that look like they grew there naturally. Stylists Samantha, Courtney, Brooklynn, and Lydia each bring their own specialty, from seamless blonde highlights to lash extensions, so every guest ends up in the right chair.

From the moment you sit down, you'll notice the salon itself: spotless, calm, and never rushed. Then you'll notice the attention, stylists who listen before they cut, explain every step of the process, and won't let you leave until your hair or lashes are exactly right. That's the reason so many first-time guests become regulars who won't sit in anyone else's chair.
