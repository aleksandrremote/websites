About Manna Flooring

Manna Flooring is a general contracting crew built around one guy who shows up the same afternoon you call, tape measure in hand, and has a real quote in your inbox by the next morning. Samuel and his team handle carpet, tile, hardwood, and full bathroom remodels, from an economy 5-year carpet you plan to upgrade later to a custom tile shower that looks like it belongs in a magazine.

What people notice most is how little drama is involved. Materials get shuffled, schedules get compressed, and somehow the job still lands ahead of the deadline, clean floors, no leftover dust, no surprises on the invoice. It's the kind of work that gets a crew invited back for the next project, whether that's a primary bathroom, a rental unit, or a wet bar nobody was expecting to build.

Common questions

How fast can you actually get started?

Samuel typically comes out to measure the same day you call and has a written quote ready by the next morning, and he's known for shifting material orders around to hit tight deadlines like holidays.

Do you only install high-end materials?

No. Manna Flooring works with every budget, from an economical 5-year carpet meant to hold you over to premium tile and hardwood, and they'll tell you honestly which option fits your plans.

Can you handle a full bathroom remodel, not just flooring?

Yes. The team has taken on complete bathroom gut renovations, fire-damage restoration flooring and tile, and even custom builds like wet bars, all managed by the same crew from start to finish.
