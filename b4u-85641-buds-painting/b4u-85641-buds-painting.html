About Bud's Painting

Bud's Painting has been putting brushes to Bangor homes and businesses for years, and Buddy's still the one showing up on the first day with a color plan and a start date he actually keeps. Whether it's a cedar shingle exterior stained entirely by hand or a set of cathedral ceilings that need a steady eye, this crew knows Benjamin Moore's color lines well enough to talk you through Wish Gray versus Symphony Blue before you ever pick up a brush.

People hire Bud's Painting once and then call back for the next project, because the crew shows up when they say they will, communicates every step, and doesn't leave until the job matches what was promised. They've painted hotel exteriors under a summer sun, repainted whole offices without disrupting the workday, and turned a fading yellow house into a Symphony Blue showpiece, all with the same unhurried, hand-done craftsmanship and a standing guarantee that you walk away happy.

Common questions

Yes. Buddy works directly with Benjamin Moore's color line and has helped homeowners land on shades like Wish Gray, Symphony Blue, and Crème Naturelle for their homes.

How long does an exterior painting or staining job take?

Most exterior projects, including full cedar shingle staining, are completed in under a week, even during the hottest stretch of summer.

Do you take on commercial jobs, not just houses?

Absolutely. Bud's Painting has painted hotel exteriors and office interiors, working around guests and staff to get the job done without disruption.
