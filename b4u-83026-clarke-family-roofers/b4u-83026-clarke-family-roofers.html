About Clarke Family Roofers

Clarke Family Roofers has built its name on the kind of jobs other crews turn down: century-old Federal Heights homes with box gutters that have to be installed just right, interlocking metal roof panels that need a repair specialist instead of a generalist, and commercial tear-offs where a busy parking lot and patients coming and going can't slow the work down. Ruben walks homeowners through their options and their insurance claim without the sales pressure, and Edward runs the crews that do the actual tear-off, install, and cleanup.

What shows up again and again from the people who've hired them is how little they had to worry once the crew showed up. Estimates are clear and fair, timelines are kept, rotted decking gets flagged before it becomes a surprise on the invoice, and job sites get swept clean at the end of every single day. Whether it's a two-day shingle replacement or 350 squares on a commercial building, the same crew that gave the quote is the one standing on the roof.
