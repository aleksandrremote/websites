About Floors De Lis

Floors De Lis is a Waterloo flooring shop built around two people: Paul, who measures and installs, and Abby, who helps you actually figure out what you want when the sample aisle feels overwhelming. They sell and install carpet, hardwood, and everything in between, and they've covered up more dated 1970s hardwood and mismatched trailer flooring than they can count.

What people notice first is how fast a project moves once Paul is on the schedule. Customers call with an urgent need, get a quote the same day, and often have new floors down and cleaned up within a week, with the kind of clean install lines that get noticed the first time someone walks in the door.

Common questions

How fast can you actually install new flooring?

Many jobs go from quote to finished floor within a week. Paul often measures the same day you call and gets you on the schedule quickly.

I don't know what flooring I want, can you help?

Yes, that's exactly what Abby does. Bring in your rough idea and she'll walk you through options and give you a fair estimate before you commit.

Do you only do full house flooring or smaller jobs too?

Both. We've done full living rooms and hallways, single bedrooms, kitchens, and flooring for a single trailer purchase.
