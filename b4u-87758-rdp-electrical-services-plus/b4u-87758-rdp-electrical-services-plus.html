About RDP Electrical Services Plus

RDP Electrical Services Plus keeps Beckley homes and businesses running, from installing whole-house generators to rewiring a three-story historic home down to the studs. When a client needed new service, wiring, lighting, and HVAC through a full renovation including a basement and rear apartment, RDP handled the entire scope without cutting corners.

People call RDP once and keep calling for years because the work is clean, the schedule is respected, and someone actually picks up when it matters. When a main breaker caught fire and a landlord wouldn't budge until morning, RDP was on site that same evening and left a generator behind so a family's food wouldn't spoil overnight. That's the standard here, whether the job is a small repair or a full-house overhaul.

Common questions

Do you work on older or historic homes?

Yes. We've rewired a three-story historic home from the ground up, including new service, lighting, and HVAC integration, while keeping the character of the property intact.

What happens if I have an electrical emergency after hours?

We're available around the clock. If your situation is serious, like a breaker fire or total outage, we'll get someone out the same evening and can supply a temporary generator if needed.

Can you install a generator for my whole house?

Absolutely, whole-house generator installation and upkeep is one of our most requested services, and we maintain them long-term so they're ready when the power goes out.
