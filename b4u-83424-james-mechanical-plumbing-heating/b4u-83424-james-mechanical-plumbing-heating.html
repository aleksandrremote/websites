About James Mechanical Plumbing & Heating

James Mechanical Plumbing & Heating is Charlie James's operation, built one Essex Junction house call at a time since he first showed up for a family stranded without water on a dead-end dirt road with a wrecked pressure tank. He handles the full range now: failed water heaters, stubborn clogs in old Vermont pipes, pressure tank repairs for rural properties, and full bathroom rebuilds.

Call Charlie and you get a real person who shows up when he says he will, often the same day for a leak or a heater that quit overnight. Customers keep him on speed dial because he tells you what's actually wrong, looks for the cheaper fix when there is one, and leaves your bathroom or basement cleaner than he found it.
