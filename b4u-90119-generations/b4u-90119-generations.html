About Generations

Generations has built its reputation on one thing: paying attention. Missy specializes in curly hair, the kind that's been chopped and shaped wrong for years by stylists who treat curls like a problem instead of a feature, and she works exclusively with Lanza products because she's seen what they do for hair that's been through the wringer.

Clients talk about the twenty-minute phone calls before a first appointment, the way she lays out pricing before anything happens, and the follow-up call a few days later just to make sure the color or the cut still feels right. That's not a script. It's just how she runs her chair, and it's why people who found her through a product website or a friend's recommendation end up staying for years.

Common questions

Does Missy really specialize in curly hair, or is that just marketing?

It's real. She's built her cutting technique specifically around curl patterns, which is why clients with hair other stylists struggled with keep coming back.

What hair products does the salon use?

Generations uses Lanza exclusively for color and treatments, a line known for restoring hair that's been damaged by past color or heat styling.

Can I get my nails done the same visit as my hair?

Yes, plenty of longtime clients started with nails and later added hair and facials, so booking both in one visit is easy to arrange.
