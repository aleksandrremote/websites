About Figuemax Tree Service

Figuemax Tree Service is Hector's crew, working out of Runnemede on everything from a single overgrown maple to a full property of trees leaning toward the house. They remove, trim, top, and clear storm damage, and they've dropped trees pushing 80 years old without touching a fence, a shed, or a neighbor's yard.

Customers keep saying the same three things: the crew shows up when they say they will, they walk through pricing options instead of handing over one number and leaving, and when they pack up, the property looks better than before they arrived. Limbs get dropped exactly where planned, cuts are made clean to promote healthy regrowth, and the cleanup leaves no branches, no sawdust, and no mess behind.

Common questions

Can you take down a tree that's leaning toward my house?

Yes, that's a lot of our removal work. The crew plans the drop zone carefully and keeps a close eye on nearby structures, AC units, and generators before making a cut.

Do you offer options if the full job is more than we planned to spend?

We do. We'll walk through what's essential versus what can wait and put together pricing that fits, rather than a single take-it-or-leave-it number.

What happens to all the branches and debris after you're done?

Everything gets hauled away the same day. Customers consistently tell us they expected some cleanup left behind and were surprised to find none.
