About Garcia Brothers Roofers

Garcia Brothers Roofers works out of McKinney on everything from routine shingle replacements to steep, skylight-heavy roofs that most crews turn down. We've walked homeowners through hail damage insurance claims from the first adjuster meeting to the final nail, handled steel building roofs alongside standard homes, and matched drip edge colors down to the shade so a new roof actually looks like it belongs on the house.

Customers keep telling us the same thing: the crew shows up organized, explains each step before doing it, and leaves the property cleaner than they found it, gutters and all. We document jobs with drone photos before and after, finish most full replacements in a single day including haul away, and answer questions by text or in person until the homeowner feels sure about every decision, not just the one they made on paper.

Common questions

Do you help with hail damage insurance claims?

Yes. We meet with your adjuster on-site, make sure no damage gets missed on the claim, and handle the paperwork side so you mostly just pick a shingle color.

How long does a full roof replacement take?

Most full replacements, including tear-off, install, cleanup, and haul away, are finished in a single day by our crew.

Can you handle steep roofs, skylights, or metal building roofs?

Yes, we regularly work on steep-pitch homes with custom skylights and have completed both residential and steel building roofs in the same project.
