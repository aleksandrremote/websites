About THE YANKEE HANDYMAN

Anthony started The Yankee Handyman because Ocala homeowners kept getting the same runaround: contractors who quoted sky-high prices, plumbers who no-showed, and handymen who left a mess behind. He built his business on the opposite of all that, taking on everything from framing a curved pool deck to rewiring a kitchen for a new chandelier, and doing it with the same care whether the job is worth ten thousand dollars or a hundred and twenty-five.

What keeps customers calling him back isn't just the craftsmanship, though sanding every edge of a deck by hand says plenty about that. It's that he shows up the same day when he says he will, quotes a fair price and sticks to it, and hauls away the old parts and cleanup mess instead of leaving it for you. Neighbors on the Bellechase homeowners page vet contractors hard before they'll recommend one, and Anthony made the cut.
