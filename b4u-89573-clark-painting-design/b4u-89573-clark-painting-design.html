About Clark Painting + Design

Clark Painting + Design is Wes Clark, working homes across Des Moines one careful project at a time. He's the guy homeowners call when honey oak cabinets need a modern update, when 1930s plaster ceilings need patching instead of replacing, or when old wallpaper has to come off without wrecking the walls underneath.

What people notice first is how calm and clear he is walking through a bid, laying out real ideas on color and finish instead of a generic sales pitch. Then they notice the follow-through: furniture protected, timelines kept, budgets respected, and a final walk-through where the only surprises are how good everything looks.

Common questions

Do you work on older homes with original plaster and trim?

Yes, Wes has repaired plaster ceilings in homes dating back to the 1930s and matches new work to original woodwork and finishes.

Can you refinish our existing cabinets instead of replacing them?

Yes, Wes regularly updates outdated cabinetry like honey oak in place, saving the cost of a full replacement while giving the kitchen a new look.

How do you handle furniture and rooms we can't fully clear out?

Wes works carefully around furniture that can't be moved and cleans up thoroughly at the end of every job, so your space is left tidy, not disrupted.
