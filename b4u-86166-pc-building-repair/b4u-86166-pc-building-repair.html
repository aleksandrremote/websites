About PC Building & Repair

Ken runs a one-man shop in Northampton that builds custom PCs and fixes the ones other people gave up on. He's the guy who takes apart a botched build, finds the hidden problem nobody else caught, and puts it back together running better than before, at a price that doesn't feel like a gut punch.

Customers keep coming back because Ken tells you exactly what's wrong, what it'll cost, and what your options are before he does a single thing. He's found malware that other repair shops missed, replaced motherboards and CPUs to bring dead builds back to life, and more than once stayed open late or opened on a Sunday just to get someone's PC done on time.
