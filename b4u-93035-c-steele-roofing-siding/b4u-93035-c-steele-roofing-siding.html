About C.Steele Roofing & Siding

C.Steele Roofing & Siding is run by Cody Steele, a Valparaiso general contractor who's built a reputation on solving the problems other contractors won't return your call about. When a steel roof was leaking and the original installer went silent, Cody was the one who showed up, pulled the roof and siding apart, fitted new rubber flashing, and had the whole thing buttoned up in a single day.

What people notice first is how much Cody communicates. He sends photos and videos as the work progresses, handles the small add-ons that pop up mid-project without making it a whole thing, and leaves a job site cleaner than he found it. Customers hire him expecting a roofer and end up calling him back for shiplap walls, trim, decks, and interior doors because the same care shows up in every square foot he touches.
