About FAITH Moving Service

Faith Moving Service is an Edinburg crew that handles everything from a single apartment to a full household, storage transfers, and yes, even pianos. We bill by the hour, so our guys move with real urgency, getting your load in fast and careful so you're not stuck paying for an extra hour you didn't need.

People notice the same thing every time: nobody on our crew makes you feel judged for a messy closet or an overstuffed garage, and nobody rushes past your furniture without protecting it. Customers call us on short notice and still get a crew that shows up on time, communicates the whole way through, and treats wide furniture and tight hallways like a solved problem, not an excuse.
