About Evergreen Eco Clean

Kimberly Gonzales built Evergreen Eco Clean around a simple idea: your home should smell good, feel good, and never make you choose between clean and safe. She cleans with plant-based products, including her own Pur Evergreen line, so families with toddlers, pets, or sensitive noses never have to worry about what's left behind.

Ask her clients and you'll hear the same things over and over: the team shows up when they say they will, they treat your house like it's theirs, and they notice the details you didn't even ask about. They'll work around your dogs instead of making you kennel them, wash the water bowls while they're at it, and even stage the kids' stuffed animals before school lets out. It's the kind of thoughtfulness that turns a cleaning appointment into something your whole family looks forward to.
