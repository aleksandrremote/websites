About Montshire Automotive

Montshire Automotive started as the shop people call when something goes wrong at the worst possible moment, a flat on a rainy Sunday, keys locked in the truck, a trailer that's come loose on the interstate. That reputation grew into a full auto repair operation, but the same instinct is still at the core of everything they do: get there fast, fix it right, and get the driver back on the road.

Customers keep coming back because Montshire treats a bad day like it matters. Techs like Alex and Jason show up quicker than the quoted time, explain what actually went wrong in plain language, and don't disappear once the tow truck pulls away. Insurance companies including USAA route roadside calls to them for a reason, and the shop's steady 4.2-star reputation across 120 reviews is built one calm, competent call at a time.

Common questions

Do you work with insurance companies for roadside calls?

Yes, Montshire is a preferred dispatch shop for carriers like USAA and handles the paperwork directly so you don't have to chase it down.

How fast can someone actually get to me?

Most calls get a technician on scene within 30 minutes or less, and in several documented cases Montshire arrived before the state trooper did.

If I'm towed, do I have to use your repair shop?

No, Montshire will tow your vehicle to the shop of your choice, including trusted partners like Bob's Service Center, or handle the repair themselves if you prefer.
