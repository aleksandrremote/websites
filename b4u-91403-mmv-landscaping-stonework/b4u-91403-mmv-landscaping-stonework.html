About MMV Landscaping & Stonework

MMV Landscaping & Stonework is Manuel Villalta and his crew, working yards across Takoma Park for years with a specialty in hand-stacked stone walls, patios, and retaining wall rebuilds that hold up for good. Manuel designs every project himself, from a gravel pathway to a full garden overhaul, then builds it alongside his team rather than handing it off to someone else.

Clients come back year after year because Manuel actually listens before he starts drawing anything up, and he catches problems most crews miss, like a drainage issue that's been flooding a yard for years or a wall that's slowly failing. His crew shows up, works hard, stays friendly the whole time, and leaves the property spotless at the end of every single day.

Common questions

Do you handle the design work or just the building?

Manuel designs every patio, wall, and pathway himself and then builds it with his crew, so the whole project comes from one vision, not a series of guesses.

Can you fix a drainage problem, not just make the yard look nice?

Yes, solving standing water and grading issues is part of most projects MMV takes on, including one where a client's serious drainage problem was resolved as part of a full yard redesign.

Do you only take on big projects or also handle ongoing garden maintenance?

Both. Plenty of clients start with a patio or wall and keep MMV on afterward for seasonal pruning and general garden upkeep.
