About Absolute Auto Care

Absolute Auto Care is Shawn's shop, and it runs the way a mechanic's shop should: no upsells, no scare tactics, just an honest look at what your car actually needs. Customers have called with a blown AC compressor or a full brake job on a work van, gotten a quote in minutes, and had the repair finished the same day.

What people notice first is the price difference. Shawn has quoted jobs at a fraction, sometimes a sixth, of what other shops in Grand Junction charged for the exact same repair, and he still gets it done fast with quality parts. He's the kind of mechanic who calls the next day just to check how your trip went, and who'll meet you at 9pm if that's when your car breaks down.
