About Lopez Landscaping & Snow Removal

Carlos Lopez started Lopez Landscaping & Snow Removal in Conneaut with a simple idea: show up when you say you will and leave the property looking better than you promised. Whether it's a half-acre lot that hasn't been touched since the snow melted or a driveway that needs plowed at 6 AM, Carlos and his crew handle mowing, flower bed cleanouts, mulching, small tree removal, and junk hauling right alongside snow removal.

People keep bringing Carlos back because he does what he says on the timeline he gives, even on short notice. Customers talk about crews that show up within days of a call, clean up messes other companies would've quoted for weeks, and prices that stay fair even after the job turns out bigger than expected. On plow days, Carlos walks the driveway with you before he leaves to make sure it's exactly how you want it.

Common questions

Do you only take on weekly customers, or can I book a one-time cleanup?

Both. Plenty of our clients started with a single overgrown lot cleanout or flower bed clearing and later moved to weekly mowing, but a one-time job is never a problem.

How fast can you get to my driveway after it snows?

We plow on a route through Conneaut and North Kingsville and before we leave your property, we walk the driveway with you to make sure it's cleared exactly how you want it.

Can you handle small trees along with regular lawn care, or is that a separate company?

Carlos and the crew handle small tree removal as part of the same visit, so you're not coordinating two different companies for one yard.
