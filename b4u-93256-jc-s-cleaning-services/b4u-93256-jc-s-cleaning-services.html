About JC&S Cleaning Services

JC&S Cleaning Services is a family-run crew led by Juan Carlos, built on a simple idea: show up fast, quote a fair price, and do the job right in front of the customer, not behind their back. What started as gutter cleaning has grown into gutter guard installation, screen repair, and full carpet cleaning for homeowners across Gurnee and Lake County.

Customers keep coming back because Juan treats their home like it's his own. He arrives within an hour of a call for an estimate, snaps before-and-after photos so nothing is left to guesswork, and points out small problems, like a sagging downspout or a stain everyone else gave up on, before they turn into expensive ones. That kind of straight talk and follow-through is why nearly every review mentions trust before it mentions price.
