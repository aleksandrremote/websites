About Creative Flooring & Design

Creative Flooring & Design has been helping Reading area homeowners pick out and install the flooring and cabinetry that actually fits their homes, not just what's in stock. Whether it's laminate across an entire first floor, sheet vinyl in a kitchen and powder room, or a full cabinet redesign that starts with Patti walking you through options in the showroom, the work is handled by the same crew from first measurement to final install.

Customers keep coming back to us for the same reason: when something goes wrong, whether it's a burst pipe or a manufacturer defect that made a brand-new floor buckle, our crew fixes it fast and treats it like their own house. That means installers who show up on time, keep the mess contained, and won't leave until the sink, the outlet, and the last cabinet door are exactly right.

Common questions

What kinds of flooring do you install?

We install laminate, tile, and sheet vinyl flooring, and our crews handle everything from a single room to a full first floor.

Can you help design a new kitchen, not just install the cabinets?

Yes, our design team works with you in the showroom to pick cabinet styles and layouts before anything is ordered or installed.

What happens if my new floor has a manufacturer defect?

We stand behind the work: if a defect causes buckling or other issues later, we'll come back and replace it completely.
