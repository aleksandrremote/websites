About Tony Kelly Nails

Tony Kelly Nails is exactly what the name says: Tony and Kelly, working side by side in their own Surprise studio, doing the acrylics and pedicures they've spent years perfecting. Clients bring them a photo, an idea, or sometimes just a color they like, and Tony builds it out into a set that's usually better than what they pictured.

People who've been coming for four and five years keep coming back for the same reason: you tell them what you want and they actually listen, then deliver it clean and consistent every single time. Appointments run relaxed instead of rushed, schedules get worked around, and the new location gives that same personal service a lot more room to shine.
