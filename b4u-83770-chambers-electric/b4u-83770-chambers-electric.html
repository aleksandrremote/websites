About Chambers Electric

Chambers Electric is Jim, and often his son Michael, showing up in Clayton to solve electrical problems that other people either can't find or won't chase down. Jim will spend 30 minutes at your panel before he ever picks up a tool, because he'd rather understand your whole system than guess at a fix. That's how he traced a decades-old attic fan nobody knew still existed to the source of a random tripping breaker, and how he plans a level 2 EV charger install even when your panel sits clear across the house from the garage.

What 341 five-star reviews keep coming back to is simple: Jim explains what he's doing, tells you what he finds, and charges what he quotes with no surprises tacked on at the end. Homeowners call him for the obvious jobs, weekend emergencies included, and end up learning things about their own wiring nobody had ever bothered to point out before. He shows up clean, works clean, and leaves your house exactly the way he found it, minus the problem.

Common questions

Do you work weekends or evenings?

Yes. Jim has shown up on Sundays and responded quickly to weekend outages because electrical problems don't wait for business hours.

That's exactly the kind of job Jim specializes in. He walks the full route from panel to garage and plans the install around your home's actual configuration.

Absolutely. Jim regularly evaluates older homes for new owners, flags outdated wiring or fixtures, and explains honestly what needs attention now versus later.
