About Nick And Son Towing

Nick And Son Towing runs out of Ellsworth and covers the Downeast stretch of Route 1 and I-95, pulling drivers out of ditches, off shoulders, and out of trouble at every hour of the day or night. It's a real family operation, Nick and his son, not a dispatch center passing your call around to strangers.

People call Nick because he shows up fast and he shows up ready, whether it's a car out of gas four miles from the next exit or an ATV stuck somewhere no tow company wants to drive. Customers keep coming back because he treats every call like it's the only one that matters, sometimes sticking around after the hook is set just to check under the hood before you head out.

Common questions

How fast can you actually get to me?

Most calls in and around Ellsworth get a truck on scene within fifteen to twenty minutes, even on the interstate.

Do you tow ATVs, side-by-sides, or motorcycles?

Yes, Nick handles buggies, ATVs, and motorcycles with flatbed equipment made for that kind of load, not just standard cars and trucks.

Are you available overnight or on weekends?

Nick And Son Towing runs 24 hours a day, seven days a week, so a breakdown at 2 a.m. gets the same response as one at 2 p.m.
