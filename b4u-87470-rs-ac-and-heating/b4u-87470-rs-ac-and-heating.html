About Rs AC And Heating

Rs AC And Heating is Roy's company, built one honest job at a time in Murphy, Texas. When your system dies in July or your furnace won't kick on in January, Roy comes out, walks you through your real options in plain language, and gets it fixed, not just patched.

Customers keep coming back to Roy for the same reason: he tells you the truth about what's wrong and what it'll cost before he touches anything, then does exactly what he said he'd do.
