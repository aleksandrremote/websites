About Pfister Painting And Decorating

Pfister Painting and Decorating has been painting homes in Ramsey and the surrounding towns for more than fifteen years, with one client keeping them on speed dial for every job since. The work ranges from full interior repaints and exterior facelifts to spraying kitchen cabinets and patching a ceiling after a water leak, and now they're adding epoxy garage floor coatings to the list.

What keeps people calling back is the same thing every time: a quote that spells out the real cost and timeline before a drop cloth ever goes down, a crew that shows up on time every single day, and floors and furniture that get protected like they're the painter's own. At the end of each day the work area gets cleaned up completely, not left for the homeowner to deal with, and the crew treats being in your home like a privilege, not a job to rush through.

Common questions

Do you protect our floors and furniture during the job?

Every surface in the work area gets covered before we start, and we clean the space completely at the end of each day, not just when the job wraps up.

Can you spray our kitchen cabinets instead of just repainting them?

Yes, cabinet spraying is one of our specialties and gives you a smoother, more even finish than brush painting without a full cabinet replacement.

How do you handle pricing and scheduling?

You get a detailed, transparent quote up front with a clear timeline, and we stick to both, arriving on time every day until the job is done.
