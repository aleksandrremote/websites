About Advanced Building Solutions

Advanced Building Solutions is a Milford general contracting outfit built around one carpenter's ability to take a homeowner's rough idea and turn it into a finished space. Hunter leads every job himself, from full kitchen renovations to custom deck builds, and he's the one working through the tricky stuff, uneven framing, awkward layouts, mid-project surprises, until the job matches what the homeowner pictured in their head.

What keeps people calling him back is consistency. He shows up when he says he will, keeps homeowners posted as the work moves along, and quotes a fair price upfront instead of padding it later. Every job wraps with the site left cleaner than he found it, which is the kind of detail that gets a contractor recommended to the next neighbor, the next cleaning crew, the next family renovating their kitchen.
