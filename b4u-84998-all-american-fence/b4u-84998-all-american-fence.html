About All American Fence

All American Fence is run by Joe, a veteran who builds fences and gates the way he was trained to do everything else: right the first time. His wife Marion works alongside him, and together with their crew they've become the name Cayce and Columbia homeowners pass along when a fence or gate needs to be done properly.

What people notice most is how fast a quote turns into a finished job, sometimes a project quoted as three days wraps in one, and how a gate that used to drag or stick suddenly swings smooth and closes flush. Joe shows up when he says he will, walks the property with you before he ever picks up a tool, and doesn't leave until the alignment, the hardware, and the finish are all where they should be.

Common questions

Can you finish a fence job another contractor started or messed up?

Yes, it's some of the work Joe is best known for. He's stepped in on multiple projects where another contractor fell through and tidied up the repairs, finished the fence, and installed the gates from scratch.

How fast can I get a quote and get started?

Joe typically walks the property, answers every question, and has a written quote back to you within a day, sometimes with quotes for related work like a back fence or deck at the same time.

Do you only do fencing, or can you handle gates and decks too?

All American Fence handles fencing, drive-through and side gates, gate repair and realignment, and deck construction, so most yard projects can be handled by one crew.
