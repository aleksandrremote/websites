About LISA 'S NAILS

Lisa's Nails is a one-woman studio on the Texas coast where every appointment is just you and Lisa, no rotating techs, no rushed timeslots. She specializes in structured gel on natural nails, acrylic fills shaped to last, and hand-painted line work that clients travel back for, sometimes from as far as San Antonio.

What keeps people coming back isn't just the polish, it's the hour she gives to a pedicure or the custom scrub blend she mixes with her own hands instead of pulling from a bottle. Clients consistently mention feeling like they walked into a friend's house rather than a service appointment, and leaving with work so clean and precise they book their next visit before they're out the door.

Common questions

Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?

Lisa is a one-woman studio, so appointments are strongly recommended to guarantee your time slot, especially for structured gel or custom art.

What makes your pedicures different from other salons?

Lisa mixes her own natural scrubs and takes a full hour on most pedicures, so it's a real treatment and not a quick soak-and-go.

I have a broken or painful nail, can you fix it?

Yes, Lisa regularly repairs broken and damaged nails and can turn a painful nail into a clean, healthy manicure in the same visit.
