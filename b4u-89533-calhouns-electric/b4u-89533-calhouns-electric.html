About Calhoun's Electric

Calhoun's Electric has been wiring homes in Corrales for 36 years, backed by 50 years of hands-on electrical experience. That kind of tenure means fewer surprises: panel upgrades, new circuits, troubleshooting, and remodel wiring that gets done right the first time, whether the house was built last year or fifty years ago.

Customers keep coming back because Calhoun's doesn't rush to send a truck and a bill. More than one caller has been talked through a fix over the phone, at no charge, before anyone even scheduled a visit, and follow-up calls after an install are standard practice, not an upsell. When new circuits go in, they're wired to match the house so well you'd think they were original to it.
