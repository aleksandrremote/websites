About Harrison & Sons Electrical Service

Harrison & Sons Electrical Service handles the electrical work other companies turn down. Full panel upgrades, knob-and-tube rewiring in century-old farmhouses, pool light replacements, and combining multiple outdated panels into one clean setup are all in a day's work for this Atlanta crew. With a 4.8 rating across 118 reviews, they've built a reputation on both residential jobs and commercial projects, including outlet relocations, smart thermostat installs, and full wiring safety checks for offices and warehouses.

Every job starts with a fair, clearly explained quote and ends with a work area cleaner than they found it. Customers consistently mention the same thing: the crew shows up on time, walks them through the wiring in plain language, and doesn't leave until everything is tested and working. When something needs a second look, like a breaker that keeps tripping, they come back fast and make it right without a hassle.
