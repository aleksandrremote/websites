About The Shop

The Shop started as Haley's dream to build a nail and beauty studio that felt as good as it looked, and it's grown into Gig Harbor's spot for nail art, lashes, brows, and skincare. Haley, Julie, Sara, and Erica each bring their own specialty, from acrylic sets and pedicures to waxing and facials, working together like a tight-knit crew rather than a rotating cast of techs.

Clients don't just book an appointment here, they build a relationship with their tech, coming back set after set because the retention holds and the grow-out still looks sharp weeks later. It's the kind of place where you show up for a fill, catch up on life while you're in the chair, and leave looking noticeably better than when you sat down.

Common questions

How long does a set actually last before I need a fill?

Most clients go three to four weeks between fills with zero breakage, which is exactly why so many of them never book anywhere else.

Can I book nails, lashes, and brows all in one visit?

Yes, a lot of clients stack Haley, Sara, and Julie in the same appointment window so they leave completely done in one trip.

How far ahead do I need to book with Haley or Julie?

They both stay booked out, so if you have a set date in mind, especially for a wedding or event, grab your spot a few weeks early.
