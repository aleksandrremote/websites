About Rick's Towing

Rick's Towing runs out of Tempe around the clock, picking up drivers the moment their car quits on them, whether that's a dead battery in a parking lot or a full breakdown on the 101. Rick and Omar don't just hook up and haul off. They'll pop the hood, check the battery, and tell you straight if a jump or a new part will save you the tow.

Callers routinely tell us the same thing: they phoned three or four towing companies, and Rick's quote came in lowest every time, often with a truck on the way before the competition even called back. People who've never needed a tow before and never wanted to remember one end up saving the number, because when the estimate is thirty minutes and the truck shows up in fifteen, that's the kind of thing you don't forget.
