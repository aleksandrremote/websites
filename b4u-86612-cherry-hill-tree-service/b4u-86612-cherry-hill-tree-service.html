About Cherry Hill Tree Service

Cherry Hill Tree Service is run by Garrett, who shows up to give estimates in person, often the same day you call, and works the job himself alongside his wife and crew. This isn't a company that subcontracts your tree out to strangers. They've dropped 50 and 60-foot pines, wrestled a beech tree off a lot with rough access in the rain, and ground stumps flush so you'd never know a tree was there.

Customers keep saying the same three things: the quote matches the bill, the crew is careful around everything they're not supposed to touch, and the cleanup is better than expected. Garrett explains why a job needs to be done a certain way before he does it, so you're never left guessing what you're paying for or wondering if it was necessary. That's the kind of clarity people remember and pass along to their neighbors.

Common questions

Do you take on smaller jobs, or just big removals?

Both. Cherry Hill has taken on smaller jobs for neighbors who saw them working nearby and asked, and they treat those with the same care as a full property clearing.

How fast can I get a quote and get scheduled?

Garrett typically calls back the same day, comes out to quote it in person, and can often have the crew on site within a couple weeks depending on the job.

What happens to the mess after the tree comes down?

Full cleanup is part of every job, including stump grinding and, when needed, soil and seeding so the area is left ready to grow back over.
