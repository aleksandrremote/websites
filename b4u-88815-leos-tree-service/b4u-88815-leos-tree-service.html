About Leo's Tree Service

Leo's Tree Service is a Mooresville crew that's just as comfortable dropping ten mature trees off a single property as it is trimming one limb hanging too close to a roofline. Leo quotes jobs himself, usually the same day you reach out, and his crew is equipped to handle the big, tall, and tricky removals other companies pass on.

What keeps neighbors and repeat customers calling is the same thing every time: Leo shows up when he says he will, the crew works fast without cutting corners, and when they leave, the yard looks like the tree was never there except for the open space where it used to stand. Fair, honest pricing on big jobs is the reason so many people end up hiring him again for the next tree that needs to come down.
