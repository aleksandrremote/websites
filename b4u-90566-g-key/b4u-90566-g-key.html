About G-KEY

G-Key is a one-man, 24-hour locksmith operation run by Genaro out of Kingston, covering the Wyoming Valley and reaching as far as Jim Thorpe when someone's stuck. He's built a reputation on the hard jobs other shops pass on, like cutting and programming immobilizer keys for Audis and VWs that most locksmiths won't touch.

People call G-Key expecting a wait and get a knock on the window instead. Genaro drives to you, diagnoses the problem on the spot, whether it's a fob that won't program or an ignition stuck in the on position, and gets it handled that same visit at a price that doesn't sting.

Common questions

How fast can you actually get here?

Most calls get a same-hour response, any day of the week, since G-Key runs 24 hours a day.

Can you handle key fobs for harder cars like Audi or VW?

Yes, immobilizer-equipped imports are a specialty, though tricky cuts like an Audi A4 can take a couple hours to get programmed correctly.

My ignition is stuck and won't turn off, can you fix that on the spot?

Genaro will come to your location, diagnose the issue, and replace the ignition housing right there in most cases.
