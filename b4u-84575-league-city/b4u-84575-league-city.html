About League City

League City is a general contracting crew based in Texas City, built for homeowners who want a straight answer and a job done the way it was quoted. Whether it's a new addition, a full renovation, or a repair that's been putting off your peace of mind, we walk the whole property first so nothing gets discovered halfway through the project.
