About Brave Ink & Design

Brave Ink & Design is Ashten's studio in Rawlins, where custom tattoos get drawn freehand and built around what actually matters to you, not a flash-wall template. Clients drive in from out of state for her linework and shading, and regulars keep coming back session after session for sleeve work and detail pieces.

People walk in nervous and leave already planning their next piece, because Ashten takes the time to actually listen before the needle ever touches skin. The studio is spotless, the sterilization protocols are taken seriously, and the atmosphere is relaxed enough that clients have called it ink therapy. She's flexible enough to fit in a first-timer, a full sleeve session, or a group of friends booking together on short notice.

Common questions

Can Ashten create a custom design from my idea, or do I need my own artwork?

Just bring the idea. Ashten draws freehand and works with you to shape the concept into a finished design before your session starts.

What's the studio like if I'm getting my first tattoo?

It's clean, quiet, and relaxed, more like a calming studio than a typical shop, and Ashten takes her time so you feel comfortable start to finish.

Can my friends and I book tattoos at the same appointment?

Yes. Ashten has accommodated groups of friends in a single time slot before and can work with tight schedules when you're planning a visit together.
