About Affordable Auto Collision And Paint Shop

Steve built Affordable Auto Collision and Paint Shop on a simple idea: fix the car right, price it fair, and treat every customer like a neighbor, because in Hopewell, they usually are. Dented doors, cracked bumpers, panel replacements, even the little stuff like a busted LED strip or a window control panel, all of it gets handled under one roof.

What keeps people coming back is how Steve runs the job. He calls with real updates, not a runaround: what's damaged, what it'll cost, and where he found the best price on the part before he ever orders it. That kind of straight talk, backed by work that holds up, is why the shop sits at a 4.8-star rating built almost entirely on repeat customers and referrals.

Common questions

How long will my repair take?

Plenty of jobs, like a door repair or bumper fix, go from drop-off in the morning to pickup that same evening. Larger collision work with parts on order usually wraps within two weeks.

Do you work with my insurance company?

Yes, we can work directly with your insurer and we source parts at the best price we can find to help keep your out-of-pocket cost down.

Will the new paint actually match the rest of my car?

That's the whole point of the job. We color match to your exact factory finish so the repaired panel blends in instead of standing out.
