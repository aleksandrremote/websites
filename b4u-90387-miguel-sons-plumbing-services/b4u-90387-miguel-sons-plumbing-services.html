About Miguel & Sons Plumbing Services

Miguel & Sons Plumbing Services is the crew Chicago calls when the water heater fails, the gas line springs a leak, or the main sewer line backs up. Technicians like Edward, Josiah, Ruben, and Jerry handle everything from a cracked sink fitting to a full gas piping replacement with permits and utility coordination, and they're on call 24 hours a day.

Every job starts with a clear, itemized quote that explains the work in plain terms, and it ends with a tested system and a clean work area before anyone leaves. Customers keep coming back because a slow bathroom drain gets the same careful attention as a major gas line replacement, and the price on the estimate is the price on the final bill.
