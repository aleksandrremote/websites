About Prestige Pavers

Prestige Pavers is run by Scott and Rick out of Callaway, and they've built a name for themselves installing pavers up and down the Gulf Coast, from Panama City to Panama City Beach to Santa Rosa Beach. Pool builders hire them as a trusted sub on their own jobs, and homeowners hire them straight up for patios, driveways, fire pits, steps, and pergolas.

What keeps people calling Scott back is how the whole thing feels while it's happening. He answers the phone, stays on the project instead of disappearing after the quote, and his crew shows up clean, on time, and finishes on the schedule they promised. Customers keep mentioning the same three things: fair pricing, sharp attention to detail on lines and levels, and a finished result that looks like it was always supposed to be there.

Common questions

Do you work directly with pool builders, or only homeowners?

Both. We're a regular subcontractor for pool contractors along the Gulf Coast, and we also work directly with homeowners on patios, driveways, and other paver projects.

Can you match pavers we already have if we're extending a patio?

Yes, matching existing travertine or paver patterns is one of the jobs we get called for most, and we take the time to get the color and pattern right.

How long does a typical paver patio or driveway take?

Most residential paver projects run days to a couple weeks depending on size, and we keep clients updated on schedule the whole way through.
