About Palmer's Drywall & Painting

Palmer's Drywall & Painting is a father-and-son operation run by Wilbur Palmer and his son Jacob, taking on drywall and painting jobs across Wheeling that other crews won't touch. That includes finishing drywall someone else started, stripping decades-old wallpaper down to bare walls, and correcting botched paint jobs left behind by previous contractors.

Customers keep coming back to the same three things: Wilbur and Jacob show up when they say they will, they tape off and cover everything before a single drop of paint hits a wall, and they leave the space as clean as they found it. Prices are quoted upfront and stay fair even when a small extra project gets added mid-job, which is why homeowners like Cheryl, Julie, and Debra call them their first choice going forward.

Common questions

Will you finish drywall that I started but couldn't complete?

Yes. Wilbur has picked up jobs mid-project before, including mudding and sanding drywall someone else hung, and finished them on time with clean results.

Do you protect floors, trim, and doorways before you start painting?

Every job starts with covering and taping the work area, so there's no dust or paint mess left behind when we're done.

Can you remove old wallpaper before painting?

Yes, stripping outdated or heavily patterned wallpaper and prepping the wall for a fresh coat of paint is one of our most requested jobs.
