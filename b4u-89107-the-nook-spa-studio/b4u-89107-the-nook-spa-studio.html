About The Nook Spa Studio

The Nook Spa Studio is Sierra's cottage-style salon in Roseburg, where a single visit might turn into a pedicure, a facial, a massage, and a fresh set of nails all in one afternoon. The studio pairs real technical skill, from custom nail art to precision hair color, with a space that feels more like a friend's living room than a clinic.

People come back because the details are never phoned in. Stylists take the time to actually listen to what you want, whether that's a bold sugar-skull pedicure for Halloween or a healthier, more manageable head of hair, and they get it right the first time. Add in the studio's resident pets wandering the halls and a mimosa in hand, and it's easy to see why regulars treat their appointment like a standing tradition.

Common questions

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointments are strongly recommended since the studio books up, especially for gel manicures and pedicures, but it's always worth calling to check for same-day openings.

Can I book a hair appointment and a pedicure in the same visit?

Yes, plenty of clients combine services in one trip, like a facial and a manicure or a haircut and a pedicure, just mention it when you book so the team can plan the time.

Are the pets actually in the salon?

Yes, Sierra's own animals are part of the studio experience and most clients say they're one of the best parts of stopping by.
