About Wrate Services

Wrate Services is a Niagara Falls contractor that handles the systems most homeowners never think about until they stop working: water heaters, furnaces, AC units, heat pump systems, and the electrical panels and wiring behind them. Tom and the team don't hand off jobs to specialists elsewhere; they show up, size up the problem, and install the fix themselves.

Customers keep coming back to Wrate for the same reason: when something breaks, someone answers and shows up fast, even during a lake effect storm at night. Jobs get done cleanly, materials get hauled away, and the work is explained in plain language before a single tool comes out, which is why people who've used them once tend to call again for the next system that needs attention.
