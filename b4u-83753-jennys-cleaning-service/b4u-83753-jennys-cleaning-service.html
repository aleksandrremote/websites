About Jenny's Cleaning Service

Jenny's Cleaning Service is run by Jennifer and her husband Mike, and they've built their name on the jobs other cleaning crews turn down: post-demolition debris, drywall dust, and the kind of deep clean a house needs before it goes on the market. Custom home builders and remodeling companies bring them in stage by stage, from framing through final walkthrough, because they show up and get it right every time.

Customers keep coming back to the same words: on time, thorough, and almost obsessive about detail. Whether it's a full construction cleanup, a move-out clean so good the old owners second-guess leaving, or a same-day deep clean before listing photos, Jenny and Mike bring their own equipment and treat every house like it's the one they're about to hand over.
