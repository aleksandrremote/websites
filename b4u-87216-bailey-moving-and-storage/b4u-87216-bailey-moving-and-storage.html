About Bailey Moving And Storage

Bailey Moving And Storage is a Kewanee crew led by Chris Bailey, and they've built their name on the moves other companies dread: full households with decades of accumulated belongings, out-of-state relocations, even a restaurant's worth of kitchen equipment hauled from downtown Chicago to Macomb. If it needs to be packed, disassembled, hauled, and put back together on the other end, this is who people call.

Customers keep saying the same thing in different words: nothing got broken, nothing got lost, and the crew worked like it mattered to them too. One family had Chris and his guys move 35 years of belongings without a single ding or scratch. Another had them unloading furniture in 10 degree wind without a single complaint until the last box was set down. That's the standard here, not the exception.
