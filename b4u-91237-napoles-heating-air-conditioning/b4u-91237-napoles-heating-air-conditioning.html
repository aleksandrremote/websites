About Napoles Heating & Air Conditioning

Carlos Napoles built Napoles Heating & Air Conditioning around a simple idea: climb the ladder, find the real problem, and show the homeowner exactly what he's looking at before he touches anything. He and his son handle AC repair, maintenance, and heating system work all over Bakersfield, from stubborn rooftop units to furnaces that won't kick on when the temperature drops.

Customers keep coming back because Carlos treats every call like it matters, whether it's a $150 quick fix or a full system diagnosis that takes four hours of testing every vent in the house. He takes photos and video of the actual unit, lays out the cheap fix next to the long-term fix, and never pushes you toward the bigger job. When people say they've found the last AC company they'll ever need to call, this is why.
