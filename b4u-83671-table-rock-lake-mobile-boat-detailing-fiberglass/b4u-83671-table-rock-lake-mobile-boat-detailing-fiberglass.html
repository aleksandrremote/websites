About Table Rock Lake Mobile Boat Detailing, Fiberglass

Captain Derek runs Table Rock Lake Mobile Boat Detailing and Fiberglass Repair out of Nixa, and he's built his name on a simple rule: a boat has to be sound before it can look good. He's patched everything from a handful of stress cracks to 78 holes under a single hull, restored gel coat on boats people thought were beyond saving, and turned a 1974 Tahiti someone bought sight-unseen on eBay into a family heirloom.

Customers keep coming back because he tells them the truth before he tells them a price. If a repair is cosmetic, he says so. If it's a safety issue, he stops you before you put the boat back in the water and explains exactly why. He handles pickup and delivery for owners who live out of town, keeps you updated with photos while the work is underway, and more than once has come in under the estimate he gave. That's rare enough in this business that people notice, and they tell their friends.

Common questions

Do I have to bring my boat to you, or can you come to me?

Most detailing and many repairs are done right at your dock or marina on Table Rock Lake, and for bigger fiberglass jobs Derek can arrange transport to and from his shop.

I have some small chips and scratches in the gel coat. Do I need a full repaint?

Usually not. Derek can match and blend gel coat repairs into the existing finish so the boat looks new without the cost of a full repaint or wrap.

Can you help if I need to file an insurance claim for hull damage?

Yes, Derek has worked directly with customers' insurance companies on gel coat and fiberglass claims and can document the damage and repair for you.
