Common questions

How do you know if a tree is actually damaging my house?

Kevin inspects the root system and cross-checks it against foundation cracks inside the home, so you're making the removal decision with real evidence, not a guess.

Can you come out the same day for a dangerous tree?

Yes. If a tree is unstable, we'll brace it, get the right equipment, and often complete the removal, stump grinding, and cleanup within hours of your call.

Will you try to save my tree instead of just removing it?

Always. Removal is the last option we offer, and we'll tell you honestly when trimming or treatment is the better call for the tree's health.
