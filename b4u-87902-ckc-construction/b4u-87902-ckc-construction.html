About CKC Construction

CKC Construction is run by Les, a Vidor general contractor who builds porches, hangs fencing, repairs windows, and rebuilds bathrooms from the subfloor up. He's the guy who walked a customer through three competing bids and won the job by simply explaining what he was doing and why, then showed her every step before moving to the next.

Customers keep coming back because the crew shows up when they say they will, even in the middle of Texas summer heat, and finishes fast without cutting corners. One family added extra work mid-project on a tub and shower replacement and the crew rolled with every change and still made it look like it was planned that way from day one.

Common questions

Do you handle small repairs or just big remodel projects?

Both. Les has done everything from sealing a few exterior windows to full porch builds and bathroom subfloor rebuilds, so no job is too small or too big.

How do you handle pricing and bids?

You get a clear, itemized bid before any work starts, and Les explains exactly what he's doing and why every step of the way, no surprises at the end.

Can you add on to a project once it's already started?

Yes, several customers have added extra work mid-project, like upgrading a tub and shower combo, and the crew adjusts without missing a beat.
