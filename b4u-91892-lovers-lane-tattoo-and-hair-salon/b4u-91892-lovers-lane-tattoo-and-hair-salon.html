About Lovers Lane Tattoo And Hair Salon

Lovers Lane Tattoo and Hair Salon has been part of the neighborhood in Loveland for years, with Tyler handling the tattoo chair and Stephanie running the styling side up front. Tyler builds custom pieces straight from a photo you bring in, from first tattoos to full sleeves people call their sticker book, and Stephanie is known around town for razor shaggy bobs and color work she dry cuts first, then refines wet.

People come back because the work holds up and the shop feels right the second you walk in. Tyler has taken in Sunday walk-ins on days the shop was technically closed, sat with clients through emotional tribute pieces, and made first-timers feel completely at ease. Stephanie does the same up front, adjusting a cut until every detail spot is exactly how you want it, in a space that's colorful, plant-filled, and never sterile.

Common questions

Do I need an appointment for a tattoo, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome and Tyler has squeezed people in same day, even outside posted hours, but booking ahead guarantees your spot for bigger pieces.

Who cuts hair at the shop, and what's their specialty?

Stephanie handles all the hair services, known for razor shaggy bobs and color, and she'll dry cut first before finishing wet to nail the details.

What kind of tattoos does Tyler do best?

Custom work built from your own photos, memorial and tribute pieces, and fine line detail that builds into full sleeve collections over time.
