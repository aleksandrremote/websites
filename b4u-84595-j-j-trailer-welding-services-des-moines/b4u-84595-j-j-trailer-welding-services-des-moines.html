About J&J TRAILER & WELDING SERVICES DES MOINES

J&J Trailer & Welding Services is John's operation, and lately his stepson's too, built around one idea: when your trailer breaks, you don't have time to wait around. They handle broken leaf springs, snapped spindles, cracked frames, flat tires, and custom fabrication work that most shops won't touch, and they do it on-site, in your driveway, at the boat ramp, or wherever you got stuck.

People call J&J when they're stranded 600 miles from home or standing at a boat ramp on a Saturday with a busted trailer and nowhere else to turn. What sticks with customers isn't just that John shows up fast, it's that he tells you the price upfront, gets it right the first time, and has you rolling again the same day, whether that's thirty minutes later or a few hours.

Common questions

Do you actually come to where my trailer broke down?

J&J is open 24 hours every day, and John has personally taken calls on Saturday afternoons and weeknights to get people moving again.

Absolutely. John has repaired everything from vintage bass boat trailers to RAGBRAI support buses, and he builds reinforcements stronger than the factory original.
