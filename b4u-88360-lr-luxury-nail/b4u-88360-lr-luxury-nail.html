About LR Luxury Nail

LR Luxury Nail sits in Albany as one of the biggest, newest nail salons in the 518, and it shows the moment you walk in. Clients come in with inspiration photos for elaborate designs and walk out with exact matches, thanks to a team that includes standout techs like Ivy, Emily, Mickey, and Sunny who each bring their own specialty to the chair.

What keeps people coming back is the consistency. Nails hold their shape and shine for weeks, pedicures come with a real leg and foot massage in chairs clients rave about, and the salon itself stays immaculately clean no matter how busy the schedule gets. It's the kind of place where a quick color change feels routine and a full custom set feels like an event.

Common questions

Do you take walk-ins or is it appointment only?

Both are welcome, but booking ahead is smart since LR Luxury Nail tends to fill up fast, especially on weekends.

How much extra does a custom design cost?

Pricing depends on the complexity of the design, generally running an extra $30 to $45 on top of your base gel service.

What's included with a pedicure here?

Every pedicure level comes with a foot and leg massage in one of the salon's massage chairs, with higher tiers adding extra treatment time and steps.
