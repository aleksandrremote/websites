About Creative Edge Custom Concrete Curbing

Creative Edge Custom Concrete Curbing is Jordan Whitaker's answer to the three-contractor headache. Homeowners come to him with a rocky yard, a half-finished landscaping plan, or just a rough idea, and he designs and pours decorative concrete curbing, planter beds, and low-maintenance layouts that tie the whole space together instead of patching it piece by piece.

Customers keep coming back to the same detail: Jordan and his crew, Daniel and Mike included, actually show up when they say they will and finish what's in front of them, whether that's grading rock before hydroseed day or picking up an irrigation job another contractor walked away from. That kind of follow-through is why so many of his clients say they'll never call anyone else for curbing again.

Common questions

Do you handle full yard design or just the curbing itself?

Jordan will design the whole layout, planter shapes, rock contrast, and all, if you hand him creative control, or he'll pour curbing to a plan you already have.

Can you work alongside a landscaper or contractor I've already hired?

Yes, we regularly partner with landscaping crews and other contractors to add curbing or finish out details on projects already in progress.

My yard is rocky, uneven, or half-finished right now. Is that a problem?

That's a normal starting point for us. We grade, shape, and design around whatever is already there, whether it's dirt, unfinished beds, or leftover rock.
