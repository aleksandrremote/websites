About Arcus Home And Flooring Vinyl

Arcus Home and Flooring is run by Enzo and Jennifer out of Royal Palm Beach, and they built their name on wood-look vinyl and laminate flooring that updates a home without the mess of tearing out old tile and grout. Beyond floors, the team takes on full kitchen and bathroom remodels, painting, and closet renovations, so one call covers an entire home update instead of juggling three different crews.

Homeowners keep pointing to the same things: the team shows up when they say they will, communicates clearly from the first estimate through the final walkthrough, and fixes what needs fixing without being asked twice, whether that means ordering extra material overnight or driving back out to repair a single strip. It's the kind of steady, honest follow-through that turns a one-room job into a repeat call for the next remodel.

Common questions

Do you prep the floor before installing new vinyl or laminate?

Yes, we remove old thinset, level the subfloor, and patch trouble spots so your new flooring lays flat and holds up long term.

Can you run a remodel if I'm not around to oversee it?

We've coordinated full bathroom remodels for owners who were out of town, keeping them updated every step from Enzo and Jennifer directly.

Do you only do flooring, or bigger renovations too?

Flooring is where we started, but we also handle full kitchen and bathroom remodels, painting, and closet work as part of the same project.
