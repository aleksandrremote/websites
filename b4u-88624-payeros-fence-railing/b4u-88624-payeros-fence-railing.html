About Payero's Fence & Railing

Payero's Fence & Railing is a Lindenhurst crew built around Nathan, who's spent his career turning bare or tired-looking yards into finished property lines homeowners are proud to show off. Whether it's a full privacy fence, a run of aluminum railing, or a straight teardown-and-rebuild, that's the work this crew does every day, not a side project bolted onto some bigger construction outfit.

Customers keep pointing to the same handful of things: the old fence and every scrap of debris hauled away before the crew ever leaves, a fair price given upfront with no surprises, and a finished yard that's often wrapped up in a single day. More than a few Lindenhurst homeowners have called Nathan back for a second and third fence on the same property, which says more about the work than any ad could.

Common questions

How long does a fence installation actually take?

Most residential jobs, including full removal of the old fence, are finished in a single day, though larger properties can take a bit longer.

Do you take away my old fence, or is that extra?

Removal and haul-away of the old fencing and all debris is included, so you're not left with a pile of scrap wood to deal with.

What's the difference in choosing wood versus vinyl?

Wood gives you a classic look you can stain or paint over the years, while vinyl costs a bit more upfront but needs virtually no maintenance after it's installed.
