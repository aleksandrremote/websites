About Scarborough Home Renovations

Scarborough Home Renovations is a Dublin, Georgia renovation company built around Jason Scarborough's eye for design and his crew's ability to execute it. Whether it's reworking a kitchen layout to fit a standard 30 inch range, adding butcher block counters and a dishwasher, or taking an entire house from outdated to current, Jason handles the kind of projects that need both a plan and skilled hands to pull it off.

What keeps homeowners calling back is simple: Jason tells you what's happening every day, not just at the start and the finish. The crew shows up when they say they will, cleans up after themselves, and catches the small stuff before it becomes a problem, which is exactly why so many first-time clients end up handing them the next project too.
