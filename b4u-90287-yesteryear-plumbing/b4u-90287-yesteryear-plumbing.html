About Yesteryear Plumbing

Yesteryear Plumbing is Spencer's business, and Spencer is a master plumber with more than 15 years in the trade, working on everything from century-old Baltimore rowhomes to newer builds. He's the one who shows up, diagnoses the problem, and does the work, whether that's a failed sump pump during a storm, a water heater on its last leg, or a stubborn drain clog that won't quit.

People who call Spencer once tend to keep his number for good. He explains what's actually happening with your pipes before he touches anything, quotes a fair price and sticks to it even when the job runs longer than expected, and leaves the work area cleaner than he found it. That combination of straight talk, steady hands, and follow-through is why customers describe him as their plumber for life, not just someone they called once.
