About West Tech Pool And Spa

West Tech Pool and Spa keeps Chico's pools clean, balanced, and ready to jump into, whether it's a decades-old backyard pool fighting off falling leaves from mature trees or a brand new above ground pool that just needs its first real chemistry lesson. Technician Mak has built a reputation for taking pools from dark green to sparkling in about a week, and then keeping them that way for good.

Customers stick around because Mak treats every pool like it's his own, closing gates behind him, explaining what he's doing and why, and never pushing equipment or chemicals nobody needs. He shows up on schedule, handles pumps and filters when they act up, and manages the tough stuff, tree debris, heavy swim traffic, dogs who treat the pool like their personal lake, without the water ever slipping out of shape.

Common questions

We have mature trees and dogs that swim every day, can you actually keep up with that?

Yes, this is exactly the kind of pool Mak specializes in. Regular skimming and balanced chemistry handle the leaf litter and heavy swim traffic without the water turning cloudy or green.

Our pool has turned green, how fast can you actually fix it?

Most green pools clear up within about a week once we get a proper treatment plan going, and we'll keep it that way with ongoing weekly service afterward.

Do you service above ground pools or just in-ground ones?

We service both. Above ground pools need their own approach to filtration and balancing, and it's something we handle regularly.
