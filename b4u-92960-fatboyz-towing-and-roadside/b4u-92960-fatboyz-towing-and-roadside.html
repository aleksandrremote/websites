About Fatboyz Towing And Roadside

Fatboyz Towing And Roadside is Daniel's operation, and he's the one who shows up whether you're stuck on the shoulder of Highway 441 in a downpour or you've dropped a wheel off a back road at 2am. He runs recovery for everything from sedans to a 36-foot RV, and if your car isn't worth fixing anymore, he'll make you a fair offer on it right there.

People call Fatboyz because the job actually gets finished, not just the tow. Daniel has hauled cars straight to the shop and then shuttled the owner to their hotel so they weren't left standing around, and he's pulled an RV free from off-road terrain without leaving a mark on the vehicle or the road. Customers keep coming back because the price quoted is the price paid, and nobody gets talked in circles.
