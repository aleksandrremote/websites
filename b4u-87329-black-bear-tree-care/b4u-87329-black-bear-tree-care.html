About Black Bear Tree Care

Black Bear Tree Care is Aaron's operation, and he runs it the way he'd want a tree crew to work on his own property. He shows up with modern, well-maintained equipment including a bucket truck, and he's taken down everything from three-story spruces leaning on garages to fifteen-plus trees tangled around power lines on a single property.

What customers keep coming back to is how little disruption there is. Aaron and his wife Lori work as a team, they explain exactly what they're going to do before they start, and when they're done the flower beds are untouched, the debris is gone, and the yard looks like nothing ever happened there but sunlight. With a 4.9 rating built entirely on word of mouth, that consistency is the whole business.

Common questions

Can you take down a tree that's leaning toward power lines?

Yes, that's a big part of what we do. Aaron uses a bucket truck and modern rigging to remove trees safely near power lines, garages, and houses.

Will you leave a mess in my yard?

No. Every job includes full cleanup, from felled logs down to the sawdust, and we're careful around gardens, fences, and anything else on your property.

I'm not sure if my tree needs to come down. Can you just take a look?

Absolutely. Aaron will evaluate the tree's health and give you an honest recommendation, whether that's removal, pruning, or leaving it alone.
