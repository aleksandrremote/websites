About Bellevue Cleaning Experts

Bellevue Cleaning Experts is a residential cleaning team led by Marina, built around one idea: do the parts of the job nobody checks, not just the parts that show. That means baseboards, blinds, range hoods, and the tops of cabinets get the same attention as the counters and floors, whether it's a one-time deep clean, a post-renovation job, or a maintenance visit that happens every two weeks.

Clients who started with a single deep clean end up staying for years, and a lot of them found the company because a friend recommended it after seeing the results firsthand. The products are strong enough to actually deep clean but safe around kids and pets, the pricing matches the quote with no surprises at the end, and the team treats furniture and electronics like it's their own house they're working in.
