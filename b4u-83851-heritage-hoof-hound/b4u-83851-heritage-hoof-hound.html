About Heritage Hoof & Hound

Heritage Hoof & Hound is Owyn's fencing and gate crew, and word around Lake City is that he's the guy who shows up, quotes it straight, and gets a fence in the ground before most companies would've called you back. Whether it's a chain-link run around two acres, a cedar privacy fence, or an automatic gate wired to open on command, the work gets built to hold up, not just look good on install day.

Customers keep coming back for the same reason: estimates arrive fast, usually with more than one option, and the crew starts on time and stays until it's right. When a job hits a snag, like a property line surprise mid-install, Owyn talks it through and solves it instead of leaving it half-finished. Properties get left clean, dogs get room to run safely, and more than a few customers have already booked him for their next stretch of fence.
