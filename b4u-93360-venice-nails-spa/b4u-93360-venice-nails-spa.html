About Venice Nails Spa

Venice Nails Spa is a Killeen nail salon built around one guy named K-K who genuinely loves what he does. He's spent years mastering the kind of detailed, complex nail art that most salons wave off as too much work, and regulars come back specifically because he can take a photo you show him and actually recreate it on your hands.

People don't just get a manicure here, they get talked into a good mood while it happens. Whether you're a longtime regular or you wandered in from the Marriott next door needing your toes done before a wedding, you get the same treatment: fast, accommodating, and a little bit of fun conversation thrown in for free.

Common questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Plenty of guests staying at the Marriott next door stop in without an appointment, and we do our best to fit you in the same day.

Can you copy a nail design I found online or on Instagram?

That's actually one of the things we're known for. Show K-K the photo and he'll recreate the design, down to the small details.

Are you open on Sundays?

Yes, we're open Sunday from 11 AM to 7 PM in addition to full hours every other day of the week.
