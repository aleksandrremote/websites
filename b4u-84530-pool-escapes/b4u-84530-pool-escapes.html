About Pool Escapes

Pool Escapes started because too many pool owners in Alabaster couldn't get a straight answer or a callback when something went wrong. Patrick and his crew specialize in the jobs other companies put off: motor and pump replacements, salt cell diagnostics, and full liner installs picked from a real catalog, not a guess.

What people notice first is that Patrick doesn't rush the conversation. He'll walk you through your salt cell readings, send photos of liner options before you commit, and quote the job before he ever picks up a tool. Customers mention the same thing every time: he says what he'll do, shows up when he says he will, and leaves the pool deck cleaner than he found it.
