About Baxter's Soft Wash And Surface Cleaning

Mr. Baxter runs his soft wash business out of Oxford, working roofs, siding, decks, driveways, and even barns across Calhoun County. He's built his reputation on jobs where the customer was staring down a roof replacement or a full repaint, and a proper wash turned out to be all it needed.

The pattern in how people talk about him is the same every time: he shows up when he says he will, he doesn't rush a job to get to the next one, and he treats a customer's siding or concrete like it's his own property. He's even finished a wash in the rain rather than push it off, which is the kind of thing that turns a one-time customer into someone who calls him every year.

Common questions

Will soft washing hurt my roof or siding?

No. Soft washing uses low pressure paired with the right cleaning solution, so it lifts algae and grime without stripping shingles or siding like a hard pressure wash can.

What kind of cleaners do you use?

We use all-natural, non-toxic cleaners on siding and softer surfaces, so your home comes out clean without a heavy chemical smell.

Do you still work if the weather isn't perfect?

We've completed full jobs even in the rain to keep a customer's schedule on track, and we plan around Alabama weather whenever we can.
