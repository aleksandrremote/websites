About Hernandez Painting

Hernandez Painting has spent years earning its 5-star reputation around New Hope one meticulously prepped room at a time. Andres and his crew handle everything from popcorn ceiling removal and drywall repair to full interior repaints and exterior jobs on homes that haven't seen a fresh coat in decades.

What keeps people calling back isn't just the paint, it's what happens before the paint goes on. Floors get covered, furniture gets protected, downspouts get detached so there's no unpainted strip left behind, and every surface gets the prep work that makes the finish actually last. Add in fast, clear communication by text or email and a crew that shows up every day until the job is done, and it's easy to see why so many clients call them again for the next project.

Common questions

How do you prepare a space before painting?

We scrape peeling paint, patch drywall, remove fixtures like downspouts when needed, and cover every floor and piece of furniture before we open a single can of paint.

What kind of paint do you use?

We paint with Sherwin-Williams, which holds up well in Minnesota's swings between humid summers and freezing winters.

Can you handle a home that hasn't been painted in years?

Yes, we regularly take on interiors and exteriors that are decades overdue for a repaint, and our prep process is built for exactly that kind of job.
