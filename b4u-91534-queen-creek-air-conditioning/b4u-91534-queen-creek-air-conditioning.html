About Queen Creek Air Conditioning

Queen Creek Air Conditioning is Craig's company, and it runs the way he does business: straight answers, fair prices, and no scare tactics. Whether it's a dead blower motor, a failing capacitor, or a system that's just tired after twenty years in the Arizona sun, Craig diagnoses the actual problem before he ever talks about replacement.

People call Craig because he picks up the phone, even on a Saturday afternoon when the house is already pushing 90 degrees, and he'll walk you through a fix over the phone if that's all it takes. When he does need to come out, he's usually at your door in under 45 minutes with the part already in hand, and he explains what's wrong in plain English before he touches a thing.
