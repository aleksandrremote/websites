About Chuy D'Painter

Chuy D'Painter has spent years painting homes across Westminster, from full interior remodels to complete exterior repaints, plus power washing for siding and post-storm cleanup. He's the guy homeowners call for the hard stuff, like the tight corners and awkward trim around Eric Linneman's remodeled master bath and closet, and the guy Stephanie Miller kept coming back to for exterior work and power washing after the Marshall Fire.

What keeps customers calling him back is simple: he quotes the job once and that's the price, he shows up exactly when he says he will, and he treats trim, corners, and closets with the same care as the big open walls. Mark Haas and Stuart Browning both point to the same thing, a fair price locked in from the start and a crew that actually shows up on schedule, which is why Stuart says he almost never recommends anyone else but recommends Chuy without hesitation.
