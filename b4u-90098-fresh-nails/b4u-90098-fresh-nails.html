About Fresh Nails

Fresh Nails has been a fixture on the Hempstead nail scene for years, built around one idea: every set should look exactly like what the client pictured. Whether it's Jenny sculpting gel extensions to a precise shape or Alice hand-painting a custom design for prom night, the goal is always the same clean lines, sharp edges, and color that reads exactly right.

Regulars keep coming back because the shaping never wavers from visit to visit, and the polish still looks sharp weeks later. Clients notice the difference in the little things, the way a tech takes an extra few minutes to get a cuticle line perfect, or double checks a nail shape before moving to the next hand, and that attention is what turns a one time visit into a standing appointment.

Common questions

How long does sculpted gel actually last?

Most clients get several weeks out of a full sculpted set before they need a fill, which is why it's become our most requested service.

Do custom nail art designs cost more than a regular manicure?

Detailed designs like the ones we do for prom or special events take more time and skill, so pricing scales with how intricate the design is, we'll always quote you before we start.

Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but if you want a specific tech like Jenny or Alice, especially for a detailed design, it's best to call ahead since we're closed Tuesdays.
