About Salon Bes

Salon Bes is built around one thing: getting your hair exactly right, even when three other stylists couldn't. Bes specializes in untangling messy, multi-toned color jobs and turning them into something you'd actually pay full price for at a high-end salon, minus the high-end price tag. His wife Rita works alongside him, and together they've made this small Plano salon feel less like an appointment and more like a place where people know your name.

Clients notice the same thing every time: he listens before he touches a single strand, works fast without ever making you feel rushed, and somehow the color always turns out better than what you pictured in your head. It's why Rebecca Ware has been sitting in his chair for over ten years, and why first-timers walk out already booking their next visit. Come in a stranger, leave part of the family that hangs around after their appointment just to talk.

Common questions

My hair is a mess from a previous salon. Can you fix it?

Yes, color correction is one of Bes's specialties, so bringing in hair with old highlights, uneven tone, or grown-out color is exactly the kind of project he loves taking on.

How far in advance do I need to book?

Bes stays busy, especially for color consults, so it's best to book a week or two out, though it never hurts to call and ask about a sooner slot.

I'm not sure what I want, can you help me figure it out?

Absolutely, most clients come in with a vague idea or a photo and leave with something even better because Bes takes the time to talk through what will actually work for your hair and face shape.
