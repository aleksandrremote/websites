About Lovely's Landscaping

Lovely's Landscaping works on residential properties across Paradise Valley, handling everything from foundation planting beds and paver walkway resets to routine lawn care and seasonal cleanups. We plan every job around the site itself, checking sun exposure, soil condition, and how the space actually gets used before a single plant goes in the ground.

Customers keep telling us the same thing: the crew shows up prepared, explains each step in plain language, and leaves the site cleaner than they found it. That kind of consistency is why so many of our regular lawn care clients eventually call us back for bed installations, paver repairs, and after-storm cleanups too.

Common questions

Do you take on small problem spots, like one patchy side yard, or only full landscape jobs?

Both. We regularly handle isolated fixes like soil amendment and reseeding a single patchy strip alongside larger front yard bed installations.

Will the plants you choose actually hold up in Paradise Valley's heat?

We pick material based on the sun exposure and mature size of each specific spot, mixing drought-tolerant and evergreen plants so beds establish and last.

Do you only do one-time installs, or ongoing maintenance too?

Both. Plenty of clients start with weekly mowing and edging, then add on a paver reset or a new planting bed once they see how we work.
