About Mai Lashes & Nail Spa

Mai Lashes & Nail Spa sits right in the heart of Yeadon, and it's become the place locals request by name, and by nail tech. Whether it's an intricate 3D gel full set for a birthday, precise French tip lines, or a full lash refresh, the work here is detailed, deliberate, and never rushed.

Regulars talk about the same thing over and over: technicians who actually listen, adjust the shape or color as many times as it takes, and treat every set like it matters. The salon stays clean and welcoming from open to close, and if something isn't quite right, the team fixes it, no arguments, just a set you're proud to show off.

Common questions

Can I get a same-day appointment for a 3D gel full set?

Walk-ins are welcome and often seen quickly, but calling ahead for a 3D gel full set gives you the best shot at your preferred tech and time.

How long does a full set with lash extensions take?

Most combined full set and lash appointments run about 1.5 to 2 hours, though intricate 3D designs can take a bit longer to get right.

What if I'm not happy with how my nails turned out?

Let the team know and they'll make it right, whether that means adjusting the shape, the color, or the whole set.
