About Prime Auto Painting

Prime Auto Painting is a New London body shop built around one obsession: paint that matches so perfectly nobody can tell your car was ever damaged. Dave, Jose, and the crew have repainted everything from a classic Mustang to a vintage Jaguar, and they've talked more than one insurance adjuster out of totaling a car that still had life left in it.

Customers keep coming back because the work holds up and the price doesn't punish you for needing a repair. Drop your car off Monday and it's not unusual to have it back by Wednesday, freshly painted, buffed, and detailed, with the kind of attention to detail that fixes problems other shops left behind.
