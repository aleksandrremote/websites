About Davefixmypcplease

Dave runs a one-man computer repair operation out of Avondale, and he's built his name on doing the work himself, right the first time. Whether it's a desktop caked in years of dust, a laptop with a cracked display, or a machine that won't boot, he diagnoses the problem fast and fixes it faster.

Customers keep coming back because Dave answers within minutes, quotes a fair price upfront, and holds to it even when a job turns out more complicated than expected.
